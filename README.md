About:
Detects the type of soil used in a land and suggests with seeds that grows the best in the soil. Next, it sows and waters the suggested seeds for the particular soil from a collection of 4 different seeds for 4 different types of soil, and sows the seeds in the perfect climate. Displays the soil type and the suggested seed type on the display. Stops automatically when the land is covered.
